Adjective

Relevant

REL-uh-vunt
Listen
Meaning

Directly connected to the subject or situation being discussed.

Use it when

Deciding whether information belongs in a discussion.

Avoid it when

Relevant does not mean interesting.

Professional context

"Please include only the information relevant to the decision."

Conversational context

"That point is relevant to what we're discussing."
